Result Update · Q4FY26

Lenskart Solutions (LENSKART)

ADD 21 May 2026 Holding period: 12 months

Result Update

Current Market Price Rs.487 as on 21 May 2026
Fair Value Rs.560 DCF / target price
Potential Upside +15.0% Fair Value vs CMP

Rationale

  • Lenskart posted strong revenue growth of 41% yoy, driven by 44% yoy growth in the India business and 35% yoy growth in the international business.
  • India’s gross margin was flat yoy and EBITDA margin expanded 622 bps yoy, sharply ahead of estimates.
  • International gross margin/EBITDA margin increased 144/111 bps yoy and also beat estimates.
  • We upgrade FY27-28 EBITDA estimates by 5-6% as we bake in higher revenue growth and margin expansion.
  • This, coupled with the roll-forward, drives a revised DCF-based FV of Rs560 (Rs525 previously). Retain ADD.

Positives

  • Strong growth driven primarily by higher volumes in both India & international.
  • Healthy margin improvement in both India and International businesses.
  • Net addition of 604 stores in FY26; long-term margin ambition of ~25%.
  • Balance sheet healthy with FCF generation.
  • We model revenue growth of 22%/21% for India and international businesses, respectively, over FY26-29.
  • We model revenue CAGR of 23% and a PAT CAGR of 48% over FY26-29.

Negatives

  • Raw material costs. The broader geopolitical environment has put pressure on cross-currency rates. This has flowed through to the cost of imports.

EBITDA - Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization; EPS - Earnings Per Share; CAGR - Compound Annual Growth Rate; FCF - Free Cash Flow; DCF - Discounted Cash Flow; bps - basis points; yoy - year on year.
